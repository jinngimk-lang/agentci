# 0002 — Evidence-Driven Growth

## Decision
Only create public-facing growth drafts when a validated artifact meets explicit thresholds.

## Context
Autonomous posting without evidence creates spam and reputational risk.

## Alternatives
- Daily posting quota: rejected.
- Release-only announcements: rejected because benchmarks/research can be more useful than releases.

## Evidence
Policy choice; thresholds are owner-editable defaults rather than claims about market truth.

## Revisit when
The owner has enough publication history to evaluate whether trigger thresholds are too strict or too loose.
